Changelog

Updates to TimeBlocks for Windows. Newest first.

TimeBlocks 1.0.2 October 16, 2026

  • Next up for you on Day view: local recommendations, why each block fits, up to three other options, and Choose Another Task (filters, inspect, pin / defer / not a fit).
  • Your current context: available time, energy, and focus goal, with an editor and a short info dialog.
  • Energy prompts on the mini timer and Day view (bolt icon, grey outline answers, skip).
  • Optional importance on Today rows (high / medium / low).
  • Next up card polish: sparkle heading, Recommended badge, flag and timer icons, coloured importance on Today rows.
  • Planned vs actual Difference column uses green / red / amber for positive, negative, and zero.
  • Running break shows one reminder line with a small icon (water, stand up, restroom, step outside, window), not a “next focus” line.
  • Starting focus from the recommendation card or the task dropdown picks the closest timer preset (25 / 30 / 60) for the block’s planned time; presets stay editable while idle.
  • Check for updates no longer opens the Store listing in a browser; status text, then the rating prompt; OK opens the Store review flow.
  • Daily reminder: focus chime, in-app banner, and tray notification when the app is hosted in the desktop shell.
  • Success chime when a break ends (updated break and success sounds in the desktop app).

TimeBlocks 1.0.1 October 12, 2026

  • Mini timer can be dragged from the surface; Day and Plan drag from the header.
  • Day, Plan, and Hide stay on the right of the header.
  • Resize the mini timer from the corners; size bands for clock-only, clock + task, and full mini controls. Day view stays fixed size.
  • Clearer status dot (green for focus, amber for break) with a short pulse on the mini timer.
  • Chimes when a focus or break segment starts.
  • Day progress bar and “blocks completed” for today’s work.
  • More reliable taskbar name and install behavior; website upgrades no longer wipe app data.
  • Installer builds publish automatically for download.

TimeBlocks 1.0.0 October 7, 2026

  • First public release.
  • Floating mini timer, Day view, and Plan for tomorrow.
  • Smooth transition between the mini timer and Day.
  • Local day storage, scheduling, and planned vs actual report.
  • Hide to the system tray.
  • Windows installer packaging.
